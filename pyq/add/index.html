    1. 1 — Save the PDF

      It is built in your browser from the pages you scanned. Nothing has left your device yet.

    2. 2 — Send it in

      Opens a pre-filled issue on GitHub. You will be asked to sign in if you are not already — that is how the paper gets credited to you, and there is nothing else to fill in. Drag the PDF you just saved into it and press submit. A bot checks the details, commits the paper and opens a pull request for a maintainer to review.
