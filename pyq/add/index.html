    Submit it

    1. 1 — Save the PDF

      It is built in your browser from the pages you scanned. Nothing has left your device yet.

    2. 2 — Send it in

      Opens a pre-filled issue on GitHub, already signed in as you. Drag the PDF you just saved into it and press submit — that is the whole job. A bot checks the details, commits the paper and opens a pull request for a maintainer to review.
